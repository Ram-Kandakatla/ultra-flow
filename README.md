# Flow

<img src="flow/icons/icon-192.png" alt="Flow app icon" width="96" height="96">

**A calm little pipe puzzle for your phone. Connect the matching dots, fill every square, beat your best time.**

### [▶ Play now: ram-kandakatla.github.io/ultra-flow/flow](https://ram-kandakatla.github.io/ultra-flow/flow/)

Free, no ads, no sign-up. Works offline once it has loaded, and it installs on your home screen like a real app ([steps below](#add-flow-to-your-home-screen)).

## How to play

1. Pick a shape, then a level.
2. Drag from a dot to its matching partner to lay a pipe.
3. Pipes can't cross, and **every cell on the board must be filled** to win.

Every board is generated fresh and always has a solution, so there's always a next puzzle.

## What's inside

- **4 board shapes:** Square, Circle, Ring and Plus.
- **5 levels:** Easy (5×5), Medium (7×7), Hard (9×9), Expert (11×11) and Godlike (13×13).
- **Points and records:** faster solves earn more points, and your best time is kept for every shape and level.
- **Hints:** stuck? Spend 10 points on a hint, or show the full solution.
- **A shop to spend points in:** backgrounds (Ocean, Forest, Dunes, Sakura, Aurora, Synthwave, and a sky that follows the time of day), dot styles (Glossy, Ring, Gem, Star) and win effects (Ripple, Confetti, Rainbow, Fireworks).
- **Light and dark mode** with a one-tap switch.
- **Private:** your progress is saved on your device only.

## Add Flow to your home screen

Flow is a web app, so there's nothing to download from an app store. Open the [live game](https://ram-kandakatla.github.io/ultra-flow/flow/) first, then:

### iPhone / iPad (Safari)

1. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up) in the toolbar.
2. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Leave the name as "Ultra Flow" and tap **Add**.

### Android (Chrome)

1. Tap the **⋮ menu** at the top right.
2. Tap **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**).
3. Tap **Install** (or **Add**) to confirm.

### Desktop (Chrome or Edge)

Click the **Install** icon at the right end of the address bar, then click **Install**.

Open Flow from its new icon and it runs full screen with no browser bars, even offline.
